---
title: Third-party SDKs
sources: [sdk-policy, third-party-sdks]
last_reviewed: 2026-10-30
verified: summary only
---

- Declare in the Data safety form the data that third-party code collects or shares, not only your own code's data.
- Use SDKs only from sources you trust and keep them updated.
- Review the SDK guidance before adding an advertising, analytics or authentication SDK.
