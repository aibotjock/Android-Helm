---
title: Store listing text and images
sources: [store-listing, dev-policy]
last_reviewed: 2026-10-30
verified: checked against source text
---

- App title: 30 characters or fewer.
- Images and text must not show price or promotional claims (for example percentage discounts or "free for a limited time").
- Do not show store performance or ranking claims (for example "App of the year" or "#1").
- Content must be suitable for a general audience.
- Every claim in the listing must match a feature in the app. Check this at each release.
