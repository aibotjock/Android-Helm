- Every app on Play must complete the Data safety form, including apps on closed, open or production tracks.
- Apps that collect no data still complete the form and link a privacy policy.
- Declare data collected or shared by third-party code (SDKs), not only your own code.
- The form now includes account-deletion questions. An approved form is needed for the account-deletion badge.
- A privacy policy link is required to complete the Data safety form.
- Prominent disclosure: summary only in this repository. Read the prominent-disclosure source before relying on it.
