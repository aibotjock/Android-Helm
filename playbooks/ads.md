---
title: Ads inside apps
sources: [ads, deceptive-behavior, prominent-disclosure]
last_reviewed: 2026-10-30
verified: checked against source text (ads section headings and rules)
---

- Ads are part of the app and must follow every other Play policy, including restricted content.
- Ads must be appropriate for the app's content rating.
- Ads must not imitate system interfaces (notifications, warnings).
- Ads must be clearly attributed to the app that serves them, stay inside that app, and not interfere with device buttons or other apps.
- If an ad interferes with normal use, it must be easy to dismiss without penalty.
- Location-based ads need the location rules in the personal and sensitive information policy.
