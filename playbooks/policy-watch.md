- Weekly: policy deadlines table, policy announcements, Developer Program Policy. These change most often.
- Monthly: the playbooks above, the target-API page, and the sensitive-permissions page.
- Quarterly: the full Play Console Help index. Read the new articles and the community announcements.
- Yearly: distribution agreement, Play terms, and other platform terms.
- Every announcement carries a minimum compliance window (for example 30 days). Record the window in data/deadlines.json the same day.
