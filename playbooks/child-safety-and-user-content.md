- The Child Safety Standards section of the Developer Program Policy applies to Anonymous Chat, Random Chat, Social and Dating apps, as the policy defines them.
- Those apps must publish standards that prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation, provide an in-app way for users to report concerns, take action on confirmed CSAM, comply with child-safety law, and name a child-safety point of contact for Google Play.
- Apps outside those categories are not expected to be in scope. Re-check this when the app's features change.
- The policy page gives the effective date for this section; record it in data/deadlines.json when it is confirmed for your category.
