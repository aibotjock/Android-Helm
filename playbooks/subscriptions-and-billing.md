- Digital goods and subscriptions sold in the app must use Google Play Billing (with narrow exceptions listed in the payments policy).
- Product IDs: start with a number or lowercase letter; may use underscores and periods; up to 40 characters; cannot be reused once created.
- Be transparent: state the offer terms, the price, the billing cycle, and whether a subscription is needed to use the app.
- Cancellation: the app must show how to manage or cancel, and must include an easy online way to cancel.
- Refunds: developers issue refunds in accordance with their own refund policy. A partial refund reduces the payout and the service fee share by the same proportion.
- Alternative billing and user-choice billing have separate rules and fee reductions. Enrol in the alternative billing page before using them.
- Lifetime purchases: a one-time in-app product rather than a subscription. The help article 'Create an in-app product' covers this; read it before building.
