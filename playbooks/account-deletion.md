---
title: Account deletion
sources: [account-deletion, data-safety]
last_reviewed: 2026-10-30
verified: checked against source text
---

- If users can create an account in the app, the app must let them ask for the account to be deleted.
- Provide an in-app path to delete the app account and its data, and a web link resource where users can request the same.
- When an account is deleted on request, the data associated with that account must be deleted too.
- Apps in regulated sectors may keep some data for longer, but must say so in their retention policy.
- Answer the account-deletion questions in the Data safety form.
