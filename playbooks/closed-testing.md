---
title: Closed testing for new personal accounts
sources: [closed-testing, test-tracks]
last_reviewed: 2026-10-30
verified: checked against source text
---

- Personal Play Console accounts created after 13 November 2023 must run a closed test before production.
- The closed test needs at least 12 testers, opted in continuously for 14 days.
- The same 12 testers must still be opted in when production access is requested.
- When applying, answer the questions about the closed test.
- Track the tester count and the day count from the first day of the test. The source does not describe what happens when testers drop out; check Play Console guidance when it happens.
