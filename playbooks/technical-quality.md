- Play measures the last 28 days of data against bad-behaviour thresholds.
- From February 2027: new bad-behaviour thresholds and optimization thresholds.
- From April 2027: apps must support zero-tap sign-in restore credentials.
- Pre-launch reports are generated automatically when you upload an app bundle or APK. Read them before rollout.
- Pre-review checks run automatically in Play Console to catch critical issues before you submit for review.
- Track the thresholds in Android vitals and fix regressions before a staged rollout continues.
